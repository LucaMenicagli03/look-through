2. Gross and net size

Net removes positions that one product holds in another when both legs are counted in the gross total. It is shown as a range where the issuer does not disclose the form of the position.

3. Redemption cascade

A holder loses a share of its supply and redeems its reserves. For each fund: the amount requested, how much available liquidity covers immediately, and what is left to settle on the next business day (T+1). Funds redeem at NAV; prices are not modelled.
